# Open Food Facts for Findings, Gemini only for photos

Free Gemini keys get no Google Search on current models, so web research failed with quota errors. Findings now come from Open Food Facts (free, with a product page as the Source, Source kind Community-reported), and typed Search uses its product search. Gemini is called once per photo Scan, on a Flash-Lite model, to read the photo. A photo's best guess is matched to Open Food Facts products, so the Diner confirms a real product. Dishes and loose foods that are not in Open Food Facts get Not enough info until a paid key adds web search.
