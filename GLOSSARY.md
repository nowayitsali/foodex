# Foodex

Foodex tells a person whether a food, product or restaurant dish fits their dietary restrictions, and shows the sources behind that answer.

## People and profiles

**Diner**:
A person using Foodex to check food against their own restrictions.
_Avoid_: User, customer, eater

**Restriction**:
One rule a Diner's food must satisfy, such as Halal, Vegan or Peanut-free. Every Restriction is strict: any trace of a conflicting ingredient makes the food Not safe.
_Avoid_: Diet, preference, filter

**Restriction category**:
The group a Restriction belongs to: Religious, Lifestyle or Medical.

**Custom restriction**:
A Restriction the Diner types in themselves because it is not on the list, entered under Religious "Other" or Medical "Other".
_Avoid_: Other allergy, unlisted restriction

**Restriction profile**:
The full set of Restrictions a Diner has chosen.
_Avoid_: Settings, preferences

## Scanning

**Scan**:
One check of a single food item: up to a few photos plus any details the Diner types in, researched online and judged against the Diner's Restriction profile.
_Avoid_: 360 scan, search, lookup

**Hint**:
A detail the Diner adds to a Scan that the photos may not show, such as the brand or the restaurant name.
_Avoid_: Manual info, extra info

**Verdict**:
The answer a Scan gives for one Restriction: Safe, Not safe or Unsure. When the evidence is weak, the Verdict is Unsure, never Safe.
_Avoid_: Result, rating, score

**Evidence**:
The sources and ingredients that back a Verdict, such as a halal certifier's listing or the ingredient that makes a product Not safe.
_Avoid_: References, proof

**Source**:
One web page or document cited as Evidence.
_Avoid_: Link, citation

## Nearby

**Restaurant**:
A place near the Diner that serves food, found by location.
_Avoid_: Venue, place, store

**Fitting dish**:
A menu item at a Restaurant whose Verdicts are Safe for every Restriction in the Diner's Restriction profile.
_Avoid_: Safe item, match
